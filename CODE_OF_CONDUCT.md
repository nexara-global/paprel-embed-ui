# Code of conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.

Contributors must communicate constructively, respect differing experiences and viewpoints, accept responsibility for mistakes, and focus feedback on the work rather than the person. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and other abusive behavior are not tolerated in project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when behavior is harmful to the community. Enforcement decisions will prioritize safety, fairness, and the long-term health of the project.
